ISA — Character Bible

Status: Draft for a PXE Boot Character Identity Pack model page. Not approved for publishing until reviewed.
Fictional: yes. Original made-up adult character. Not based on any real person or celebrity.
Tone: Safe-for-work. Keep this character on pxe-boot.com and in pack samples.

Name

ISA. One name, all caps in typography (ISA). No last name in pack assets.

Concept

Isa is the flagship editorial creator persona for the Character Identity Pack — a composed, glamorous adult character lead. She is the woman on the official Gumroad deposit cover: crimson leather, gold hoops, red lipstick, high black ponytail, black studio ground. Use her when the site needs a clear, high-end example of an avatar, a banner, a thumbnail, and a likeness set that is obviously the same person.

Look

  • Adult woman, apparent age late 20s to mid 30s.
  • Warm medium-tan olive skin, natural texture, not over-smoothed.
  • Oval face with softly full cheeks, defined cheekbones, a straight nose with a softly rounded tip.
  • Arched dark brows, large brown almond eyes, long lashes.
  • Full lips with smooth, bold crimson lipstick. Expression is calm and self-possessed, often a three-quarter turn with the gaze just off-camera to the viewer's right. A slight almost-smile is allowed; a wide grin is not her default.
  • Long wavy black hair pulled into a high ponytail that falls behind her left shoulder. Hairline and ponytail are locked; do not switch to loose hair or a bun on pack hero shots.
  • Large thin gold hoop earrings (smooth or lightly faceted, not heavy costume jewelry).
  • Signature outfit: a structured glossy crimson red leather blazer with wide lapels, no logos, no text. Worn as the hero look on the cover, avatar, banner, and thumbnail.
  • Likeness variations may change outfit and setting only, never the face: (1) fitted black turtleneck on a black studio ground; (2) plain ivory silk blouse in a warm daylight interior; (3) tailored black blazer over a simple black crew top, evening interior with soft amber bokeh.
  • No tattoos required. No glasses.

Personality

Composed, self-possessed, and editorial. Isa does not perform for the camera so much as hold it. Warmth is present but quiet — she is not bubbly, not sarcastic, and not distant. Confidence without bravado. She feels like the lead of a campaign: few words, clear presence, the same person in every frame. On a model page, describe her as a creator persona and character lead, not as a streamer with a bit or a lore-heavy heroine.

Style notes

  • Hero pack shots: pure solid black background, beauty key light from the front-left, gentle highlight on the cheekbone and on the leather, 85mm editorial head-and-shoulders.
  • Keep the face locked to the official cover portrait. The high-res pack master matches that face; avatar, banner, and thumbnail are crops of that master. Do not rebuild the official cover — reuse the deposit cover.
  • Accent color is crimson (#C41E3A), with gold (#D4AF37) as a secondary note from the hoops. Banner role text and the thumbnail label bar use crimson. Cover stacked letters stay white on black.
  • Role line: CREATOR PERSONA. Tagline: EDITORIAL CHARACTER LEAD. Thumbnail word: MUSE.
  • Do not add text inside generated portraits. Do not add logos. Do not show hands on pack hero shots. Keep all images safe for work: covered clothing, no suggestive posing.
  • Lighting may shift in likeness images (studio beauty light, soft daylight, warm evening bokeh), but skin tone, makeup, and face structure stay the same.

Palette

  • #C41E3A — crimson (primary accent: banner rule, role text, thumbnail bar)
  • #D4AF37 — gold (hoops and secondary accent; not required on typography)
  • #000000 — ground
  • #FFFFFF — name lettering
  • Makeup lock: bold crimson lip, warm sculpted cheek, defined dark brow

Typography on the cover, banner, and thumbnail uses Archivo (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts), variable axes weight 900 / width 125. Letters are composited in layout; do not bake typography into a generated portrait.

Usage

  • Homepage hero examples: use the 1280×720 site crops (site_example-avatar.jpg in place of images/example-avatar.jpg, and site_example-likeness.jpg in place of images/example-likeness.jpg). Do not replace images/example-voice.jpg.
  • Model page: show the four pack pictures (cover, avatar, banner, thumbnail) and this bible, with optional likeness images underneath to show the same face in other outfits.
  • These files are portfolio samples of what the $49 Character Identity Pack contains (avatar, banner, thumbnail, plus a short usage note). They are not a likeness of a real client.
  • Keep Isa on the public PXE Boot site only in this SFW editorial form. Do not cross-link her to any 18+ project or use the Keyhole P logo as a character mark on her portraits.
