MOCHI — Character Bible

Status: Draft for a PXE Boot Character Identity Pack model page. Not approved for publishing until reviewed.
Fictional: yes. Original made-up adult character. Not based on any real person or celebrity.
Tone: Safe-for-work. Keep this character on pxe-boot.com and in pack samples.

Name

MOCHI. One name, all caps in typography (MOCHI). No last name in pack assets.

Concept

Mochi is an original VTuber-style streamer persona brought into photoreal form: a bubbly lilac-haired adult host with a glowing cat-ear headset, built for just-chatting and chaos streams. She is the brightest, most playful character in the sample lineup, without becoming childish or costume-mascot.

Look

  • Adult woman, mid 20s, Korean, original fictional character.
  • Pastel lilac hair in two small space buns with loose face-framing strands. This hair is locked for pack hero shots.
  • Soft pink blush, glossy lips, a bright friendly smile. Head often in a three-quarter turn, looking at camera.
  • White over-ear gaming headset with small cat-ear shapes that glow soft pink. Headset is a locked prop for pack hero shots.
  • Signature outfit: an oversized lavender hoodie with no logos, no text, and no prints.
  • Youthful adult, not a teen and not a childlike mascot. No tattoos required.

Personality

Bright, talkative, and affectionate in a just-chatting way. Mochi brings playful chaos, quick laughter, and a lot of face energy, but she is still an adult host, not a bit that talks down to the viewer. She is easy to like, a little impulsive, and warm rather than sharp. The persona should feel like a stream someone leaves on in the background because the company is good.

Style notes

  • Hero pack shots: pure solid black background, soft beauty key light with a subtle magenta and violet rim, 85mm editorial head-and-shoulders.
  • Face is locked to a single master portrait; cover, avatar, banner, and thumbnail are all crops of that master.
  • Accent color is lilac #C9A7FF, with pink #FF7AC8 as the secondary (blush, headset glow, companion accent). Banner role text and the thumbnail label bar use lilac. Cover stacked letters stay white on black.
  • Role line: STREAMER PERSONA. Tagline: JUST CHATTING + CHAOS. Thumbnail word: LIVE.
  • Do not add platform logos, channel names, or readable UI. Do not make the cat-ear headset cartoonish or the face underage. Keep clothing casual and covered. No hands in pack hero shots. Safe for work.

Palette

  • #C9A7FF — lilac (primary accent, hair)
  • #FF7AC8 — pink (secondary, headset glow and blush)
  • #000000 — ground
  • #FFFFFF — name lettering

Typography on the cover, banner, and thumbnail uses Archivo (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts), variable axes weight 900 / width 125. Letters are composited in layout; do not bake typography into a generated portrait.

Usage

  • Model page: show the four pack pictures and this bible.
  • Use Mochi when you need a clearly playful, streamer-coded sample that is still photoreal and adult.
  • Portfolio sample only, not a real client likeness. Safe for work. No platform or brand marks.
  • Keep her on the public PXE Boot site in this SFW streamer-persona form.
