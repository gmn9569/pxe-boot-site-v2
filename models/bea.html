BEA — Character Bible

Status: Draft for a PXE Boot Character Identity Pack model page. Not approved for publishing until reviewed.
Fictional: yes. Original made-up adult character. Not based on any real person or celebrity.
Tone: Safe-for-work. Keep this character on pxe-boot.com and in pack samples.

Name

BEA. One name, all caps in typography (BEA). No last name in pack assets.

Concept

Bea is an original cozy gamer: a warm, silver-braided grandmother-type host for farming sims, tea, and slow Saturday streams. She is the softest, most welcoming character in the sample lineup — built for people who want a digital self that feels kind, unhurried, and lived-in rather than high-fashion or competitive.

Look

  • Adult woman in her late 50s, South Asian, original fictional character.
  • Warm brown skin with natural texture, soft crow's feet, a genuine kind smile.
  • Silver-streaked dark hair in a loose side braid, not a tight or formal style.
  • Round tortoiseshell glasses. Small gold stud earrings.
  • Signature outfit: a chunky mustard-yellow knit cardigan over a cream turtleneck. No logos, no text, no game merch.
  • No tattoos required. Hands are not shown in pack hero shots.

Personality

Warm, patient, and unhurried. Bea has the energy of someone who will gladly make a second cup of tea and let you take your time. Gentle humor, no sarcasm that cuts. She is a host more than a performer: welcoming, steady, and easy to sit with. She never feels rushed, trendy, or sharp-edged. Think slow Saturday mornings, farming-sim chat, and a grandmother who actually enjoys the game.

Style notes

  • Hero pack shots: pure solid black background, warm soft golden key light from the front-left (lamp-light feel), a gentle amber rim light, 85mm editorial head-and-shoulders.
  • Face is locked to a single master portrait; cover, avatar, banner, and thumbnail are all crops of that master.
  • Accent color is mustard #E0A526, with cream #F3E6CF as the secondary (turtleneck and a soft companion to the cardigan). Banner role text and the thumbnail label bar use mustard. Cover stacked letters stay white on black.
  • Role line: COZY GAMER. Tagline: TEA, FARMS + SLOW SATURDAYS. Thumbnail word: COZY.
  • Do not add game logos, console brands, or identifiable titles. Do not youth-wash her face or remove the glasses and silver in the hair. Keep clothing cozy and covered. No hands in pack hero shots.

Palette

  • #E0A526 — mustard (primary accent)
  • #F3E6CF — cream (secondary, knit and turtleneck)
  • #000000 — ground
  • #FFFFFF — name lettering

Typography on the cover, banner, and thumbnail uses Archivo (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts), variable axes weight 900 / width 125. Letters are composited in layout; do not bake typography into a generated portrait.

Usage

  • Model page: show the four pack pictures and this bible.
  • Use Bea when you need a sample that is older, warmer, and clearly distinct from Isa's editorial glamour or Tala's competitive look.
  • Portfolio sample only, not a real client likeness. Safe for work. No brand marks or game IP.
  • Keep her on the public PXE Boot site in this SFW cozy-gamer form.
