# TALA — Character Bible

Status: Draft for a PXE Boot Character Identity Pack model page. Not approved for publishing until reviewed.  
Fictional: yes. Original made-up adult character. Not based on any real person or celebrity.  
Tone: Safe-for-work. Keep this character on pxe-boot.com and in pack samples.

## Name

TALA. One name, all caps in typography (TALA). No last name in pack assets.

## Concept

Tala is an original competitive FPS player: a focused Pacific Islander competitor with a lime-trimmed jersey and a headset around her neck, built for ranked-grind streams. She is the disciplined athlete in the sample lineup — less theatrical than Mochi, less glamorous than Isa, all focus.

## Look

- Adult woman around 30, Samoan Pacific Islander, original fictional character.
- Focused, determined expression with a faint confident smile. Looking at camera, often a three-quarter turn.
- Long dark curly hair pulled into a high bun.
- A small traditional-style pattern tattoo band may be visible at the shoulder edge; do not add other tattoos, and do not invent a readable or sacred motif beyond a simple geometric band.
- Black over-ear headset resting around her neck (locked prop for pack hero shots; not worn over the ears in the hero portrait).
- Signature outfit: a plain black athletic zip jersey with lime-green trim. No logos, no text, no sponsor patches.
- Athletic, strong presence. Not stylized as a mascot or a fashion editorial.

## Personality

Focused, disciplined, and competitive. Tala does not waste words. She has ranked-or-nothing work ethic and a quiet confidence rather than trash talk. Respect for the grind, no excuses, no theatrical villain energy. She feels like someone you would want on your team: prepared, steady, and hard to rattle. A faint smile is earned, not performed.

## Style notes

- Hero pack shots: pure solid black background, crisp key light with a subtle lime-green rim, 85mm editorial sports head-and-shoulders.
- Face is locked to a single master portrait; cover, avatar, banner, and thumbnail are all crops of that master.
- Accent color is lime `#9BFF2E`, with charcoal `#1E1E1E` as the secondary (jersey and a dark companion). Banner role text and the thumbnail label bar use lime. Cover stacked letters stay white on black.
- Role line: FPS COMPETITOR. Tagline: RANKED GRIND, NO EXCUSES. Thumbnail word: RANKED.
- Do not add game titles, studio logos, team names, or sponsor marks. Do not move the headset onto the ears on pack hero shots unless a later approved likeness says so. Keep clothing athletic and covered. No hands in pack hero shots. Safe for work.

## Palette

- `#9BFF2E` — lime (primary accent, jersey trim)
- `#1E1E1E` — charcoal (secondary, jersey)
- `#000000` — ground
- `#FFFFFF` — name lettering

Typography on the cover, banner, and thumbnail uses Archivo (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts), variable axes weight 900 / width 125. Letters are composited in layout; do not bake typography into a generated portrait.
