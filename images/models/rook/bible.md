# ROOK — Character Bible

Status: Draft for a PXE Boot Character Identity Pack model page. Not approved for publishing until reviewed.  
Fictional: yes. Original made-up adult character. Not based on any real person or celebrity.  
Tone: Safe-for-work. Keep this character on pxe-boot.com and in pack samples.

## Name

ROOK. One name, all caps in typography (ROOK). No last name in pack assets.

## Concept

Rook is an original RP street-racer persona: a calm, confident night-run driver in a plain orange and black motorsport jacket. He is the cool-headed competitor in the sample lineup. No cars, tracks, city names, or racing-series marks appear in his pack — the jacket and the attitude carry the idea.

## Look

- Adult man, early 30s, Black, original fictional character.
- Short tapered fade with a crisp line-up, neatly trimmed short beard.
- Confident slight smirk, looking at camera, often a three-quarter turn.
- Signature outfit: a plain matte black and burnt-orange color-blocked motorsport-style jacket with a high collar. Completely plain fabric: no logos, no text, no patches, no badges, no flags.
- No helmet in pack hero shots. No cars or other vehicles in frame.
- Clean, sharp grooming. No tattoos required on pack hero shots.

## Personality

Cool-headed and competitive without flash. Rook does not over-explain and does not show off. The slight smirk is self-assurance, not mockery. He feels precise, night-run ready, and in control. Think a driver who already knows the line, not a loud party racer. He can be used in RP or motorsport-adjacent stories, but the public pack should stay quiet, stylish, and grounded.

## Style notes

- Hero pack shots: pure solid black background, dramatic soft key light from the front-left, a subtle warm orange rim on the shoulder, 85mm editorial magazine head-and-shoulders.
- Face is locked to a single master portrait; cover, avatar, banner, and thumbnail are all crops of that master.
- Accent color is orange `#FF6A13`, with graphite `#2A2A2A` as the secondary (jacket and a dark companion). Banner role text and the thumbnail label bar use orange. Cover stacked letters stay white on black.
- Role line: STREET RACER. Tagline: RP RACE NIGHTS. Thumbnail word: RACE.
- Do not show cars, plates, tracks, energy-drink brands, or racing-series marks. Do not add extra patches to the jacket. No hands in pack hero shots. Safe for work.

## Palette

- `#FF6A13` — accent orange (primary)
- `#2A2A2A` — graphite (secondary, jacket)
- `#000000` — ground
- `#FFFFFF` — name lettering

Typography on the cover, banner, and thumbnail uses Archivo (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts), variable axes weight 900 / width 125. Letters are composited in layout; do not bake typography into a generated portrait.
