# HEX — Character Bible

Status: Draft for a PXE Boot Character Identity Pack model page. Not approved for publishing until reviewed.  
Fictional: yes. Original made-up adult character. Not based on any real person or celebrity.  
Tone: Safe-for-work. Keep this character on pxe-boot.com and in pack samples.

## Name

HEX. One name, all caps in typography (HEX). No last name in pack assets.

## Concept

Hex is an original night-courier cyberpunk lead: a weathered, rain-soaked after-midnight courier with a pushed-up cyan visor and a neon-trimmed tech jacket. He is the late-shift professional in the sample lineup — calm, practical, and built for after-hours work rather than for the stage or the stream.

## Look

- Adult man in his early 40s, Middle Eastern, original fictional character.
- Short dark hair with grey at the temples, a neatly kept salt-and-pepper short beard.
- Serious, calm gaze; head often in a three-quarter turn.
- A thin rectangular smoked-cyan visor pushed up on his forehead (a locked prop — do not omit it on pack hero shots, and do not pull it down over the eyes unless a later approved likeness says so).
- Signature outfit: a high-collar matte black technical jacket with subtle seams and a single thin cyan light strip along the collar. No logos, no text, no symbols, no faction marks.
- Light rain droplets on the jacket shoulders are part of the hero look.
- Weathered but healthy; not injured, not dirty, not costumed as a soldier.

## Personality

Quiet, reliable, and economical with words. Hex has night-shift composure: he has already solved the problem before he talks about it. Not grim, not wisecracking, not a stoic cliché — just calm, a little weathered, and focused. Dry humor is allowed in small doses. He feels like someone you would trust with a package at 2 a.m., not like a superhero or a street brawler.

## Style notes

- Hero pack shots: pure solid black background, cool cyan rim light on one side and a faint magenta rim on the other, soft key light on the face, 85mm cinematic head-and-shoulders.
- Face is locked to a single master portrait; cover, avatar, banner, and thumbnail are all crops of that master.
- Accent color is cyan `#00E5FF`, with magenta `#FF3DBB` as the secondary rim. Banner role text and the thumbnail label bar use cyan. Cover stacked letters stay white on black.
- Role line: NIGHT COURIER. Tagline: DELIVERS AFTER MIDNIGHT. Thumbnail word: ON CALL.
- Do not add city skylines, vehicles, readable signage, or brand logos. Do not turn the visor into a full helmet. Keep the look photoreal, not illustration-heavy. No hands in pack hero shots. Safe for work.

## Palette

- `#00E5FF` — cyan (primary accent, visor and collar light)
- `#FF3DBB` — magenta (secondary rim)
- `#000000` — ground
- `#FFFFFF` — name lettering

Typography on the cover, banner, and thumbnail uses Archivo (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts), variable axes weight 900 / width 125. Letters are composited in layout; do not bake typography into a generated portrait.

## Usage

- Portfolio sample only, not a real client likeness. Safe for work. No brand marks.
