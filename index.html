Our rights-first promise

Your face, your voice, and your likeness belong to you. That's the whole model.

  • No rights grabs. We don't take ownership of, or licenses to, anything you create.
  • No platform lock-in. You aren't tied to our tools or our service to keep using what you build.
  • We sell the skill, not the content. You pay to learn, not to rent your own likeness back.
  • You stay in control. You decide where your digital self lives and how it's used.
